## Netflix Analysis — Business Recommendations

1. **Increase Series Content** — Balance the movie-heavy catalog by producing and acquiring more TV shows.
2. **Invest in New Content** — Maintain consistent new releases to sustain and grow the subscriber base.
3. **Diversify Geographically** — Acquire more titles from countries outside the USA for a global audience.
4. **Focus on Popular Genres** — Drama, Comedy, and Documentaries show the highest popularity and should receive continued investment.
5. **Produce Short Films & Mini-Series** — Cater to viewers with limited time availability.
6. **Collaborate with Popular Talent** — Leverage well-known actors and directors to attract their established fan bases.
7. **Strategic Launch Timing** — Identify peak months for TV show viewership and schedule launches accordingly.
8. **Sustain TV Show Growth** — A recent trend shows increasing demand for TV shows; maintain this investment focus.
